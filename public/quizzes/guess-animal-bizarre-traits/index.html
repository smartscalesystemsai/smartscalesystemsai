Guess the Animal (Bizarre Traits)

15 questions with answers and explanations · free to read and reuse

Evolution produces things that sound invented: a mammal that shrugs off oxygen deprivation, an amphibian that survives being partly frozen.

Questions built on genuinely strange biology have a useful property — the true answer often sounds less plausible than the distractors. That inverts the usual guessing strategy of picking whatever seems most reasonable.

Every question below shows all four options, the keyed answer, and the one-line explanation. If you want to turn these into video, the OmniFrame Quiz Studio is free and this pack is built in.

All 15 questions

Question 1

Which animal has a bill like a duck, lays eggs, and senses electricity?

  • Platypus  ← answer
  • Echidna
  • Duck
  • Beaver

Why: Platypuses are egg-laying mammals with electroreception.

Question 2

Which creature can regrow limbs, spinal cord, and parts of its heart?

  • Salamander
  • Axolotl  ← answer
  • Starfish
  • Gecko

Why: Axolotls are famous for extreme regeneration.

Question 3

Question 5
