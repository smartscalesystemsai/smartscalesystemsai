Optical Illusions & Brain Glitches

15 questions with answers and explanations · free to read and reuse

Illusions are not failures of the eye. They are the visual system doing exactly what it evolved to do: guess.

Vision does not record light levels, it infers surfaces and lighting from ambiguous data, and the inference is usually right. Illusions are the cases engineered to make a good strategy produce a wrong answer — which is why knowing the trick does not stop you seeing it.

Every question below shows all four options, the keyed answer, and the one-line explanation. If you want to turn these into video, the OmniFrame Quiz Studio is free and this pack is built in.
