Unbelievable Animal Facts

15 questions with answers and explanations · free to read and reuse

Animal trivia usually fails in one of two ways: it asks something everyone knows, or it asks about a species nobody has heard of. Neither produces the feeling of almost knowing the answer.

These questions aim at animals you are familiar with doing things you probably are not aware of — the bird that remembers a human face for years, the mammal whose fingerprints can confuse a forensic examiner. The subject is recognisable; the fact is not.

Every question below shows all four options, the keyed answer, and the one-line explanation. If you want to turn these into video, the OmniFrame Quiz Studio is free and this pack is built in.

All 15 questions

Question 1

Which bird can remember a threatening human face for years?

  • Pigeon
  • Sparrow
  • Crow  ← answer
  • Ostrich

Why: Research on wild crows shows long-lasting recognition of human faces associated with danger.

Question 2

Which insect tells others where food is by dancing?

  • Ant
  • Butterfly
  • Dragonfly
  • Honeybee  ← answer

Why: The waggle dance communicates direction and distance.

Question 3

Which animal sleeps with half its brain at a time?

  • Dolphin  ← answer
  • Seal
  • Otter
  • Manatee

Why: Some dolphins use unihemispheric sleep to keep breathing.
