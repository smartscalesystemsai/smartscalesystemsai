About Smart Scale Systems

Smart Scale Systems builds practical AI tools for two groups of people who both lose hours to work that software should absorb: managers who spend their evenings writing performance reviews, and creators who spend their weekends editing videos.

The site is run as an independent, self-funded project. There is no team, no investors, and no content agency behind it. Everything published here is written and built by one person, which is worth stating plainly because it shapes what you will and will not find on the site.

Why this exists

Most AI advice falls into one of two piles. The first is abstract — think pieces about what AI will mean for knowledge work, written by people who have not tried to use it for anything specific. The second is a list of tool names with affiliate links attached, which tells you what exists but not how to use it.

What is missing is the middle: the actual workflow. Which prompt, in what order, with what context supplied, producing what output, and what to do when the output is wrong. That gap is what this site tries to fill. Every tool here started as a problem worth solving, not as a keyword worth ranking for.

What we make

  • Free browser tools. The OmniFrame Quiz Studio turns a topic into a faceless quiz video. The hook generator drafts opening lines. The blueprint engine assembles a starter AI stack. These run entirely in your browser — nothing you type is uploaded to a server.
  • Prompt libraries for managers. Ready-to-use prompts for reviews, feedback, and planning, including a free set you can try before buying anything.
  • Published guides. A Kindle book on using AI to lead teams, plus downloadable kits sold through Gumroad and Etsy.

How we decide what to publish

A few working rules, stated so you can hold us to them:

  • We use what we publish. Prompts and workflows on this site are ones we actually run, not ones generated to fill a page. When something works only in narrow conditions, we say so.
  • Free tools stay genuinely free. The browser tools have no signup, no watermark, and no usage cap. They are supported by ads on those pages, which is why the ads exist.
  • Ads never appear on pages selling something. The homepage and paid product pages carry no advertising. Mixing a sales pitch with ad inventory is a bad experience and we do not do it.
  • Affiliate links are disclosed, always. Some links earn a commission. That never changes the price you pay, and we do not recommend tools we would not use ourselves. Full detail is on the disclosure page.
  • Corrections are made in public. If something here is wrong, tell us and we will fix the page rather than quietly delete it.

How the tools are built

The browser tools are static web applications — no accounts, no database, no server-side processing of what you enter. When you generate a quiz or a hook, the work happens on your own machine. This is a deliberate design choice: it keeps the tools fast, keeps them free to run, and means there is no store of your inputs to leak or sell.

The trade-off is that the tools cannot save your work between sessions. Export what you make before closing the tab.
